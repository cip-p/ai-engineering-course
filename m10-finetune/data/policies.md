# Lumen & Oak: customer policies

Lumen & Oak is a fictional online shop for furniture and lighting, invented for lesson 10.4. No model has seen these policies during its training, so it can only know them from finetuning or from retrieval.

## Returns

Items can be returned within 45 days of delivery, as long as they are unused and in their original packaging.

## Refunds

A refund is paid back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at the warehouse.

## Standard shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over €60. Orders of €60 or less pay €4.95 for standard shipping.

## Express shipping

Express shipping costs €12. Express orders placed before 14:00 arrive on the next business day.

## Where we ship

Lumen & Oak ships to every EU country, plus the United Kingdom, Norway and Switzerland. It does not ship outside Europe.

## Warehouse pickup

Orders can be picked up at the warehouse in Tilburg, in the Netherlands, from Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00.

## Customer support hours

Customer support is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 20:00 and Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00, Central European Time. It is closed on Sundays.

## Loyalty points

The loyalty programme is called Oak Points. Customers earn 1 Oak Point per euro spent, and 100 Oak Points can be exchanged for a €5 discount.

## Discount codes

Only one discount code can be used per order, and discount codes cannot be used to buy gift cards.

## Gift cards

Gift cards come in values of €25, €50 and €100, and they are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase.

## Damaged items

An item that arrives damaged must be reported within 7 days, with a photo of the damage. A replacement is then sent free of charge.

## Price match

If the same item is sold for less by another retailer in the EU within 14 days of the purchase, Lumen & Oak refunds the difference.

## Deleting an account

An account is deleted within 30 days of the request. Order history is kept for 7 years, because tax law requires it.

## Two-factor codes

The two-factor login code sent by text message is valid for 10 minutes.

## Payment methods

Lumen & Oak accepts credit and debit cards, PayPal, iDEAL and Klarna. With Klarna, customers can pay within 30 days.

## Warranty

Furniture has a 2-year warranty, and lighting has a 1-year warranty.
