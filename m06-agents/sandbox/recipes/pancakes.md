# Pancakes (serves 4)

- 200 g flour
- 2 eggs
- 300 ml milk
- 1 tbsp sugar
- a pinch of salt
- butter for the pan

Whisk the flour, sugar and salt. Add the eggs and half the milk, whisk until smooth, then add the rest of the milk. Rest for 15 minutes. Cook in a hot buttered pan, about a minute per side.
